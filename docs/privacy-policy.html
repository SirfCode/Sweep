Privacy Policy for Seep

Effective date: 24 September 2026

Seep is a card game published by SirfCode. You can play the game without signing in. Google sign-in is optional and is used for account-based features such as saving completed game reports and analysis snapshots.

Information We Collect

When you use Seep without signing in, completed game reports are not linked to a Google account. When you sign in with Google, we may collect and store:

  • Your Google account email address.
  • Your Google display name, when provided by Google.
  • Your Google account subject ID, which helps us identify the same account if your email address changes.
  • Completed game reports, including app version, platform, scores, winner, and summary information.
  • Game log data for completed games where the human player wins.
  • Saved deal snapshots when analysis access is enabled for your account and you choose to save a deal for analysis.

How We Use Information

We use the information above to provide account features, upload completed game reports, support game analysis, improve bot strategy, troubleshoot issues, and maintain the Seep service.

Sharing

We do not sell your personal data. We use service providers to operate the app and backend, including Google sign-in, Google Play, Render hosting, and Render Postgres database services. These providers process data only as needed to provide their services.

Data Storage and Security

Data is transmitted using HTTPS. Account and game report data is stored in backend database tables used by Seep. We take reasonable steps to protect the data we store, but no system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Ads and Payments

Seep currently does not show ads and does not offer in-app purchases, paid subscriptions, cash rewards, or redeemable prizes.

Children

Seep is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13.

Your Choices

You can play without signing in. You can also request deletion of your account data or specific game report data. See the Account and Data Deletion page for instructions.

Contact

If you have questions about this privacy policy or your data, contact us at glidekosh@gmail.com.
