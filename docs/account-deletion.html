Seep Account and Data Deletion

Effective date: 24 September 2026

Seep lets you play without signing in. If you sign in with Google, Seep creates an account record so completed game reports and optional analysis snapshots can be linked to your account.

Request Account Deletion

To request deletion of your Seep account and associated data, email glidekosh@gmail.com from the Google account you used to sign in to Seep, or include that email address in your request.

Use the subject line: Delete my Seep account.

Request Partial Data Deletion

You can also request deletion of specific Seep data, such as completed game reports or saved analysis snapshots, without deleting your whole account. Email glidekosh@gmail.com with the subject line: Delete my Seep game data.

What We Delete

When an account deletion request is verified, we delete or anonymize the Seep user account record and associated completed game reports and saved analysis snapshots where technically possible.

What May Be Kept

We may keep limited records if needed for security, fraud prevention, debugging, legal compliance, or backup restoration. Backup copies may remain for a limited period before they are overwritten or deleted according to normal backup cycles.

Response Time

We aim to respond to deletion requests within 30 days.

Contact

For account or data deletion requests, contact glidekosh@gmail.com.
