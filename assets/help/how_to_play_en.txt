Your team versus the bot team
Collect points together. Your team wins when it leads by 104 points after a deal.

YOUR TURN
Tap a card, choose a highlighted move, then confirm. Play one card each turn.

• Capture: take matching cards or groups that add up to your card. A 10 can take a 10 and a 6 + 4.
• Build: combine cards into a house worth 9–13. Keep a matching card to capture it later. Opponents can capture it too!
• Raise: increase an ordinary house while keeping a card of its new value. A pakka house cannot be raised.
• Throw: leave a card on the table when it cannot capture. The app shows your legal choices.

THE OPENING CALL
The starting player calls 9–13 using a rank in their hand. Their first move must follow that call. A = 1, J = 11, Q = 12, K = 13.

POINTS & SEEPS
Spades score their value; other Aces score 1; ♦10 scores 6. Other cards score 0.
Clear the table to earn a seep: 25 on the first play, 50 later, none on the final play. Your team needs at least 20 card points that deal to keep seep bonuses.

QUICK TIP
Protect your partner’s houses and avoid leaving an easy seep. Tap a player to see their latest capture; open Scores for the totals.

BOT DIFFICULTY
Guests play Easy bots. Signing in starts a fresh game with stronger bots. Medium and Hard are coming later.
